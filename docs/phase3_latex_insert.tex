\subsection{Planned ESP32 hardware evaluation}
The next phase plans to use a selected ESP32 board after its exact SKU, memory configuration, and test
instrumentation are recorded. The hardware team will collect input-side power, workload duration, memory,
temperature, and transport observations with an external measurement setup. No board measurements are
reported in this phase. Each configuration will include warm-up and at least 30 retained trials, including
failures and software throttling events.

Raw observations will be preserved separately from derived statistics. Any later measured parameter
profile will record the source-data hash, statistic, unit conversion, spread, profile version, and source
revision. Synthetic profiles and frozen evaluations will remain unchanged. Matched task inputs will be
replayed in simulation and on the board, with differences and uncertainty reported separately.
